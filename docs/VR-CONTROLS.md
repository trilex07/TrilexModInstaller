# VR controls

These are the default right-handed controls for the installer profiles. Custom
bindings or a different runtime profile may change what a button does. Grip
means **L1 / R1** on PS VR2 Sense and the **side grip buttons** on Meta Quest
Touch. L3 and R3 mean pressing the left and right thumbsticks in.

## Singularity VR

The manual-reload profile is tested on PS VR2 Sense. The Touch column shows
the equivalent inputs when using the compatible Touch binding.

| Action | PS VR2 Sense | Meta Quest Touch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Move / turn | Left stick / right stick | Left stick / right stick |
| Jump | Right stick up | Right stick up |
| Aim and fire / select in VR Settings | Point right hand and pull R2 | Point right hand and pull right trigger |
| Start reload / back in menus | Circle | B |
| Get ammo or support the gun | Left grip near left shoulder or front of gun | Left grip near left shoulder or front of gun |
| Weapon wheel | Hold R3, select a slot, release R3 | Hold right stick click, select a slot, release |
| Use a health item | Cross | A |
| TMD Impulse | L2 | Left trigger |
| TMD Age / Renovate | Square | X |
| Open menu | Triangle | Y |
| Steady the Kasimov scope | Bring scope to eye and hold R1 | Bring scope to eye and hold right grip |
| Recenter | L3 + R3 | Both stick clicks |

- **Centurion:** press reload to open the cylinder. Raise the muzzle to eject
  the cases. Reach behind your left shoulder and squeeze the left grip for
  fresh ammunition, insert it, then flick the gun to close the cylinder.
- **Valkyrie, Volk and Kasimov:** press reload to release the magazine. Grab a
  fresh one from behind the left shoulder and insert it. No closing flick.
- The knife is at the left hip; the standard weapon holster is on the right.
  **VR Settings** has reload hints and a tutorial. TMD actions depend on the
  abilities and energy you have in the campaign.

## The Darkness II VR

Starting with installer 1.1.2, **VR Controls** sits beside **VR Settings** in
the main menu and pause menu. Select it with the right-hand pointer and trigger.

| Action | PS VR2 Sense | Meta Quest Touch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Move / turn | Left stick / right stick left or right | Same |
| Sprint | L3 while moving | Left stick click while moving |
| Jump / crouch or stand | Flick right stick up / down | Same |
| Fire the right / left gun | R2 / L2 | Right / left trigger |
| Reload / use | Cross / Square | A / X |
| Weapon wheel | Hold R3, move your right hand toward the weapon, then release R3 | Hold right stick click, move your right hand toward the weapon, then release |
| Show objective and route | Hold Circle for 1 second | Hold B for 1 second |
| Grab / throw with the left tentacle | Left grip | Left grip |
| Slash with the right tentacle | Hold R1 and swing | Hold right grip and swing |
| Pause / back | Double-tap Triangle / Circle | Double-tap Y / B |
| Recenter / toggle VR and cinema | Click L3+R3 together / keep held for 1.5 seconds | Click both sticks / keep held for 1.5 seconds |

- **Throw:** aim at an object and squeeze the left grip to grab it. While
  holding the grip, swing your left hand toward the target and release the grip
  during the swing. If eye tracking is enabled and available, you can look at
  the object to aim the grab.
- **Objective:** keep Circle or B held to show guidance and follow the purple
  trail. A quick tap still performs the unlocked native action.
- **Two-handed gun:** move your left hand to the front of the gun and hold its
  grip to steady it. The manual VR / cinema override lasts 60 seconds.
- Native story and execution choices keep their own prompts; use the menu
  button to pause in those contexts.

## Tiny Tina's Wonderlands VR

| Action | PS VR2 Sense | Meta Quest Touch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Move / turn | Left stick / right stick left or right | Same |
| Sprint | L3 while moving | Left stick click while moving |
| Shoot / aim down sights | R2 / R1 | Right trigger / right grip |
| Cast spell / use class ability | L2 / L1 | Left trigger / left grip |
| Jump / crouch or slide | Cross / Circle | A / B |
| Reload / interact | Tap / hold Square | Tap / hold X |
| Switch weapons | Triangle | Y |
| Ping | Right stick up | Same |
| Melee | Tap R3, or hold it and swing the right hand | Tap right stick click, or hold it and swing |
| Point and select in menus, shops and map | Right-hand laser and R2 | Right-hand laser and right trigger |
| Recenter | Hold both stick clicks for about 0.8 seconds, or F9 | Same |

- Turn the left wrist toward you to see the minimap and mission tracker. Touch
  the left watch with your right hand for the pause menu, or touch the right
  watch with your left hand for the map or character screen.
- Bring the left hand toward the gun for the support grip. The right wrist
  displays ammo, health, ward and spell cooldowns.
- The default reload uses the game's animation. The optional gesture layout
  and custom `[Bindings]` in `WonderlandsVR.ini` can change this table.
